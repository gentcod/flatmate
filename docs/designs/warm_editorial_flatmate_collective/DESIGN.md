## Brand & Style

This design system crafts a grounded, emotionally reassuring sanctuary for young professionals, relocation movers, and independent renters seeking compatible home environments. Co-living is vulnerable, stressful, and emotionally fraught; the visual narrative avoids sterile transactional tropes, over-gamified dating mechanics, and clinical rental marketplaces.

The aesthetic philosophy balances **Warm Editorial Serenity** and **Architectural Tactility**:
- **Tone:** Grounded, candid, calm, and intellectually mature.
- **Attitude towards Trust:** Radical transparency without alienation. Verification states avoid punitive red warning indicators, preferring frank, dignified, matter-of-fact documentation badges ('Self-reported', 'Government ID confirmed', 'Landlord reference on file').
- **Visual Personality:** Feels like an architectural journal crossed with an intimate domestic collective—textured mineral whites, deep botanical greens, sun-baked clay, and unhurried whitespace.

## Colors

The palette establishes psychological ease through organic mineral tones and forest canopy hues, completely eschewing harsh digital blues and abrasive neons.

### Canvas & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base (`#FBF9F5`):** Warm limestone canvas used for default page backgrounds.
- **Surface Muted (`#F4EFEB`):** Soft parchment container fill used for nested cards, secondary sidebars, and input field tracks.
- **Surface Elevated (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure warm white reserved for active interactive cards, modal layers, and focused dropdown overlays.

### Brand Primaries & Accents
- **Deep Eucalyptus (`#1E3F35`):** Primary brand anchor used for primary actions, critical navigation anchors, and dominant interactive highlights.
- **Forest Canopy (`#2D5A4C`):** Tertiary interactive hover state, active tab indicators, and balanced visual containers.
- **Sun-Baked Terracotta (`#C86D51`):** Intentional editorial accent reserved for high-affinity match highlights, key callouts, milestone badges, and selective notification pips.

### Neutral Slate Hierarchy
- **Slate Text Primary (`#1C2522`):** Grounded deep stone slate delivering high contrast and fatigue-free legibility.
- **Slate Text Secondary (`#475450`):** Soft charcoal-sage for subtext, interface labels, and metadata.
- **Slate Text Subtle (`#73807B`):** Muted botanical grey for timestamps, inactive tab labels, and caption metadata.

### Borders & Dividers
- **Warm Sand Border (`#E5DFD5`):** Structural hairline divider for baseline card framing and horizontal rules.
- **Sage Hairline (`#D7DFDB`):** Contextual border for highlighted or verification card segments.

## Typography

The typography couples the intellectual warmth of **Newsreader** (used for humanizing headlines, editorial intros, and bio pull-quotes) with the clarity of **Plus Jakarta Sans** (used for structural UI, metadata badges, data drivers, and inputs).

- **Display & Headline Levels:** Set in Newsreader with optical sizing. Use italic styling sparingly for personal statements, lifestyle mantras, and flatmate values.
- **Body & Data Levels:** Plus Jakarta Sans ensures tabular consistency across pricing metrics, transit intervals, habit breakdowns, and verification logs.
- **Editorial Micro-treatments:** Use `label-sm` with subtle uppercase tracking exclusively for contextual categories (e.g., "LEASE DURATION", "CLEANLINESS CADENCE", "SLEEP SCHEDULE").

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on a 12-column desktop grid with a maximum content container of 1280px, flanked by generous exterior canvas margins (`margin: 3rem`) to avoid claustrophobic dashboard density.

### Breakpoints & Canvas Scaling
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters. Split-screen arrangements give 7 columns to browse profiles/spaces and 5 columns to contextual breakdowns (living habits, compatibility drivers, and house agreements).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns, 20px gutters, 32px canvas margins. Sidebars collapse to anchored bottom sheets or contextual drawer overlays.
- **Mobile (Up to 767px):** 4 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, 20px (`1.25rem`) outer canvas margins. Card padding steps down smoothly from `space-xl` to `space-md`.

### Rhythmic Rules
Section blocks strictly rely on `space-xl` (40px) or double-spacing (80px) for thematic division, allowing listings and individual profiles breathing room reminiscent of modern print architectural portfolios.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy prioritizes **tonal layering** and **low-contrast outlines** over synthetic deep drop shadows. The system mimics stacked tactile sheets of fine Japanese paper and linen mats.

### Elevation Architecture
1. **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#FBF9F5` base plane. No shadow.
2. **Level 1 (Resting Cards & Panes):** `#FFFFFF` surface bordered by a 1px hairline stroke of `#E5DFD5`. Shadow is ambient, diffused, and warm: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(28, 37, 34, 0.04)`.
3. **Level 2 (Interactive Hover & Active Cards):** 1px border shift to `#D7DFDB` with extended feather: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(28, 37, 34, 0.07)`.
4. **Level 3 (Modals, Slide-over Drawers, Popovers):** `#FFFFFF` surface with crisp framing: `0 16px 40px -8px rgba(28, 37, 34, 0.12)`.

Backdrop filters on sticky navigational utility bars use subtle glassmorphism (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) with 88% alpha tint of `#FBF9F5` to maintain environmental grounding during vertical traversal.

## Shapes

The design system embraces an organic, approachable geometry:
- **Cards, Panes & Sheets:** Styled uniformly with 16px (`rounded-lg` / `1rem`) corner radiuses, eliminating sharp corporate edges.
- **Input Fields & Interactive Controls:** Standardized at 10px to 12px corners for tactile snugness.
- **Badges & Trust Status Chips:** Pill-shaped (`9999px`) to visually differentiate metadata elements from interactive card containers.
- **Media & Photos:** Profile headshots utilize circular framing (`rounded-full`) or soft-corner portrait frames (16px radius), preserving personal intimacy.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid Deep Eucalyptus (`#1E3F35`) background, `#FBF9F5` text, 12px border radius, 0 1px 2px rgba(0,0,0,0.05). On hover: transitions to `#2D5A4C`.
- **Secondary (Terracotta Accent):** Solid Warm Terracotta (`#C86D51`) background, white text. Reserved for primary conversion events ("Request to Connect", "Schedule Viewing").
- **Subtle / Outline:** Transparent fill, 1px `#E5DFD5` border, `#1C2522` text. Hover brings a gentle `#F4EFEB` tint.

### Trust & Verification Badges
- **Philosophy:** Avoid alarmist red error icons and celebratory gamified trophies.
- **Self-Reported Badge:** Background `#F4EFEB`, text `#475450`, border 1px solid `#E5DFD5`, with neutral hollow dot icon.
- **ID / Reference Confirmed:** Background `#EBF1EE`, text `#1E3F35`, border 1px solid `#C2D2CB`, with understated solid shield check icon.

### Compatibility & Match Driver Bars
- **Score Representation:** Displays advisory metrics (e.g., "88% Alignment") alongside transparent drivers.
- **Track & Fill:** The container track is a 6px tall rail in `#E5DFD5`. The fill utilizes a solid eucalyptus bar (`#2D5A4C`) for standard traits and terracotta (`#C86D51`) for pivotal lifestyle matches (e.g., "Sleep schedule match", "Work from home harmony").
- **Drivers Breakdown:** Accompanied by inline micro-labels highlighting commonalities without absolute value judgments.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text & Select Fields:** Crisp `#FFFFFF` background resting on `#FBF9F5` pages, 1px solid `#E5DFD5` border, padded by `0.75rem 1rem`. Focus state reveals a 1.5px solid `#1E3F35` ring without harsh default browser glow.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 20px controls with a 1.5px `#73807B` border. When checked, elements transition to `#1E3F35` fill with a crisp `#FFFFFF` checkmark.

### Listing & Flatmate Profile Cards
- **Structure:** 16px corner radius, `#FFFFFF` container with 1px `#E5DFD5` border.
- **Card Header:** Media carousel or portrait thumbnail paired with an editorial italic pull-quote summary.
- **Card Body:** Clear 2-column key/value breakdown for logistics (Budget, Neighborhood, Move-in window, Pet status) paired with subtle status badges.
- **Footer:** Direct match score indicator and candid bio snippet.
